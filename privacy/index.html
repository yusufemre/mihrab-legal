Mihrab Privacy Policy

  • Effective date / Yürürlük: July 12, 2026
  • Publisher / Yayıncı: Vibe Coding Studios
  • Contact / İletişim: vibecodingstudios@gmail.com

This policy applies to Mihrab on Android and iOS, including its home-screen and lock-screen widgets. Mihrab is designed without advertising SDKs, cross-app tracking or a mandatory user account. Most settings and worship records remain on the device.

1. Information processed

  • Location: precise or approximate device location when permission is granted, or coordinates of a city selected manually.
  • Preferences: language, theme, city, madhab, calculation method, time format, notification choices and widget configuration.
  • Local worship records: prayer check-ins, qada plans, streaks, tasbih counts, Quran reading progress, favorites and similar app activity.
  • Cached content: prayer calculations, Quran and hadith content, market prices and other data cached to improve speed and offline use.
  • Purchase records: product and transaction status supplied by Apple App Store or Google Play for optional, consumable support purchases. Mihrab does not receive payment-card details.

2. Location, qibla and nearby mosques

Location is used to calculate prayer times and qibla, and to find nearby mosques when you request that feature. Mihrab does not use location for advertising or continuous movement tracking. You may select a city instead of granting location permission. Nearby-mosque searches may send coordinates or a selected city to OpenStreetMap services (Nominatim and Overpass). Choosing directions opens Apple Maps or another map application, whose own privacy terms then apply.

3. Notifications and audio

With permission, Mihrab schedules prayer-time, advance reminder, Friday and spiritual-content notifications on the device. Notification choices, including individual prayer toggles and sound selection, are stored locally. Adhan audio is used only for prayer-time notifications configured to use it; advance reminders use the applicable reminder sound. iOS and Android may delay or suppress notifications because of system settings, battery policies or device limits.

Quran audio may continue while the app is in the background and may appear in system media controls. Audio is streamed from the Islamic Network Quran CDN unless already cached by the operating system.

4. Widgets and shared app storage

On iOS, the app and its WidgetKit extension use an Apple App Group to share only the information needed to render Mihrab widgets, such as selected language and theme, prayer times, next-prayer countdown, daily verse, hadith or dua, and tasbih count. This shared container stays on the device and is not a Mihrab cloud account. Removing a widget does not necessarily delete the underlying app preferences; uninstalling the app removes them subject to operating-system behavior.

5. Sharing and generated images

Mihrab can generate share cards for verses, hadiths, duas and prayer times. Nothing is shared automatically. A file leaves the app only after you choose Share and select a recipient or third-party application. The selected recipient's privacy policy applies after the transfer.

6. External content and data providers

  • AlQuran Cloud and Islamic Network CDN: Quran text, translations and audio.
  • Hadith API content distributed through jsDelivr/GitHub: hadith-library content.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim and Overpass: nearby-mosque searches.
  • Apple Maps or installed map providers: navigation requested by the user.
  • finans.truncgil.com, Binance, Stooq and Metals.live: gold, silver and exchange reference data for informational zakat calculations.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play: optional in-app support purchases and store transaction processing.

Network requests expose normal technical data such as IP address, device network information, requested URL and request time to the relevant provider. Mihrab does not send prayer check-ins, qada records, tasbih history or Quran progress to these content providers.

7. Advertising, analytics and tracking

The current Mihrab release contains no advertising network, no Firebase Analytics, no cross-app tracking SDK and no sale of personal information. Mihrab does not create behavioral advertising profiles. Store operators may provide aggregated installation, purchase and crash information under their own policies. If Mihrab later introduces analytics, advertising or account-based cloud services, this policy and store disclosures will be updated before release.

8. Storage, security and retention

Local records are stored in the app's protected storage and iOS App Group container where widgets require access. Network requests use HTTPS. Local data remains until you clear it, reset the relevant feature or uninstall the app, subject to device backup and operating-system behavior. Cached remote content may be replaced when refreshed. Mihrab currently has no server-side user account or account-deletion database.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Use a manually selected city instead of device location.
  • Change or revoke location and notification permissions in system settings.
  • Enable or disable prayer and spiritual reminders inside Mihrab.
  • Remove local records by using available reset controls, clearing app data or uninstalling the app.
  • Contact us to ask privacy questions or report an incorrect disclosure.

Because Mihrab does not maintain user accounts or a server-side worship-history database, we normally cannot identify or delete device-local data remotely.

10. Children

Mihrab is a general religious utility and is not directed at collecting personal information from children. It does not require an account for core use. Parents and guardians should manage device permissions, purchases and sharing controls where appropriate.

11. International processing and changes

External providers may process network requests in countries outside your own under their respective policies. We may update this policy when features, providers or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
